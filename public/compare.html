Card Rails Stablecoin (USDC)
ModelPull: merchant requests the debitPush: customer initiates the transfer
FlowAuth → Capture (2 steps)Single transaction (1 step)
SettlementT+2 (sometimes T+1)~15 seconds

FinalityProvisional for 120 days (chargeback window)Irreversible once confirmed
ChargebacksYes: cardholder can disputeNo dispute mechanism
RefundsStandard flow, same rail in reverseNew transfer initiated by merchant
CustodyBank (customer funds) / Acquirer (merchant funds)Depends on model: self-custody, custodial, or shared (MPC). This demo uses dev-controlled shared custody via Circle.
Merchant feesInterchange 1.5–3%None (gas paid by sender)
Sender feesNone for the cardholderGas (~$0.01–0.50 depending on network)
IntermediariesIssuer → Network → Acquirer → ProcessorBlockchain (validators only)
Cross-borderFX surcharge 1–3%Same cost, no borders
ReconciliationStatements, batches, clearing filesPublic ledger in real time (block explorer)
